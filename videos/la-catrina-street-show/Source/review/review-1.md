**Silhouette / proportions:** The long low coupe stance, wheelbase/overhang balance, wheel size, gently crowned hardtop and rear-deck relationship convincingly translate the atlas. Pillar/window corners and some bumper contours remain more angular, but the overall silhouette is strong.

**Body surfacing / panel fit:** Broad shoulders, panel boundaries, roof crown and predominantly closed wheel wells form a coherent finished game body. Residual flaws are soft lower swage channels, thin/abrupt trim connections and a small pink/bright upper opening in the front well. Close-up finish remains short of the atlas's polished surfacing.

**Paint / material fidelity:** Candy, shell pink, plum, champagne ornament and cameos clearly carry the original identity, with visible fine-scale finish variation. The close-up grain can read as orange peel rather than delicate metalflake, and the gold scallops are more repetitive/bold than the original filigree. Shell-pink depth and candy reflections are also limited by presentation lighting.

**Wheels / chrome / cabin / detail:** Wire-wheel weave, narrow whitewalls, tire relief, ribbed silver rear fascia, lamp detail, cameos and plum pleating make a convincing game-scale detail set. Dashboard, seat/trim edges, bumper corners and some small fittings remain noticeably simplified.

**Visible mechanism / component plausibility:** Front arms, separate dampers, supported rams, rigid rear axle/links, pump rack, straps, cables and curved bay enclosures read as a coherent installed mechanism. Tall rectangular support towers and simplified cup/link interfaces remain less integrated and less compact than the atlas. This is visible game-model plausibility, not manufacturing certification; service removal is not penalized.

## Remaining visible flaws

1. **Small pink/bright upper well opening, angular inner-well surfaces, soft lower swages and abrupt trim/window connections remain.** Evidence: `wheel-detail.png`, `front-detail.png`, `side.png`, `cabin.png`. Refinement: Close the remaining upper inner-well sightline with a body-following return and refine lower swage and trim transitions. Preserve the current overall silhouette.

2. **Paint grain can look like orange peel, while ornament is bold/repetitive relative to the atlas.** Evidence: `wheel-detail.png`, `cabin.png`, `top.png`, `front-detail.png`. Refinement: Make the fine finish read as restrained metallic sparkle under clear gloss, retain smoother broad reflections, and preserve more delicate layered champagne linework at game-view scale.

3. **Dashboard, seat/trim edges, small fittings and bumper corners are still basic.** Evidence: `cabin.png`, `rear-detail.png`, `front-detail.png`. Refinement: Refine the dashboard/instrument surround, upholstery edge finish and chrome corner curvature toward the atlas's slender rounded forms.

4. **Tall rectangular support towers and simple cup/link-end interfaces remain schematic.** Evidence: `front-corner-wheel-off.png`, `rear-links-wheel-off.png`, `bay-detail.png`. Refinement: Bring tower/bracket silhouettes closer to the atlas's compact integrated mounting forms and add visibly seated cup/link details while preserving readable supported connections.

These remain visible despite the narrow pass. They are refinement opportunities, and the pass is not a claim of exact visual equivalence to the atlas.
