Each directory retains the original delivery receipt. Paths inside those
receipts identify the original build/session files; they are historical
provenance, not a requirement for playing these copies. The latest suspension
and three-vehicle films also include their encoded review sheets.

All three films use native 960x600 footage at 30 FPS. The forest races use
scripted planar paths; the later suspension/cabin film uses the coupled
playground physics. Reconstructed audio and simplified vehicle/cabin behavior
remain subject to human review. See the current root handoff for open work.

The separate narrated walkthrough and editable project are under
`videos/playground-showcase/`. Its native video clips and final MP4 are local
only on `main`; the editable source, audio and documentation remain versioned.
