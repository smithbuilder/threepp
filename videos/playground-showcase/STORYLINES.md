# Storylines considered

1. **Proving ground walkthrough — chosen.** Follow the same vehicle system from contact and body motion to difficult terrain, jumps and wind, ending with the reusable catalog and measured checks. Real footage supplies the world; restrained soil/paint colors and labels explain the action. Warm instrumental underscore and a calm narrator give the motion room.
2. **Three-vehicle field trial.** Repeat every obstacle for Safari, buggy and Baja as a matched test matrix. Strong comparison, but much longer and more repetitive than this showcase needs.
3. **Excitebike day in the desert.** Build an energetic stunt reel around ramps and landings, revealing the quiet engineering underneath at the end. Fun, but the jumps would overshadow the suspension trails and wind facility.

Chosen direction: the walkthrough best answers what was built and why it matters. No invented before/after footage or race framing.
