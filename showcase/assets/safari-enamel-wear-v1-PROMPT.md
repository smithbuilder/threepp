# Safari enamel wear v1

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, normal built-in mode. No CLI,
external paid API key, source photograph or manufacturer pixels were used.
The user reference guided the palette and type of wear; it was not supplied as
an input image. This is albedo texture data, not a rendered vehicle or gameplay.

Nominal source scale: one square metre. The project maps the selected detail
onto the physical body atlas, with body-position masks controlling dust density.
Normals remain the restrained original enamel microfinish. Roughness and
clearcoat masks are derived material data, not copied from image lighting.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: square game PBR BASE-COLOR / ALBEDO texture, not a product photograph, not concept art.
Primary request: one original flat, seamless-looking tile of warm ivory automotive enamel on a well-used off-road rally vehicle. The image is ONLY the surface colour data, filling every pixel edge-to-edge, straight orthographic view, no perspective and no visible object or panel boundary. One square metre of real material scale.
Material: smooth cured cream-ivory automotive paint, intact and clean across about 75 percent of the tile. Authentic fine surface history: sparse tiny stone chips with subdued grey primer at their centers, hairline scratches, softened scuff edges, scattered dry sandy-brown mud flecks, thin dust accumulations and irregular gentle rubbed patina. Detail at several scales, from submillimetre enamel grain through millimetre chips to a few diffuse 2-8cm dust deposits. Rich photographic irregularity, restrained and maintained, not a wreck. Smooth broad paint areas remain readable. A few warm brown flecks should be visible; avoid making everything nearly uniform beige.
Palette: ivory paint around RGB218,210,188; neutral-grey primer, dry earth-brown wear; no orange rust.
Lighting: albedo data under perfectly uniform diffuse illumination; absolutely no baked specular highlights, no cast shadows, no gradients from a light source, no directional shading or ambient occlusion. Texture colour only.
Composition: no central subject, no pattern motif, no repeated stripes. Distribute detail naturally. Compatible opposite edges for tiling, similar value and detail density at all borders.
Avoid: stucco, plaster, stone, concrete, peeling house paint, cracks, rusty metal, big tears, raised chunky flakes, dramatic grunge, strong dark bands, brush strokes, perspective, photographic borders, panel seams, screws, bolts, tools, labels, logos, numbers, lettering, watermarks, charts, comparison views.
```
