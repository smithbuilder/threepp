## Current visible assessment

- **Silhouette:** The long hood/deck, hardtop opening, low body and partly covered rear wheel form a coherent atlas interpretation in both side views and the top view. The roof perimeter and its ends still read as a thin cap, with more angular pillar transitions than the reference. The category reflects the overall coupe shape rather than exact dimensional certification.
- **Body:** The closed panels, rounded arch returns and lined front arch read as fitted parts. Lower-body stampings remain broad and soft in `wheel-detail.png`, with abrupt end transitions. Hood/deck outer corners and body-to-bumper joins remain more sharply segmented than the continuously rounded reference surfaces. These are close-view surface limitations; image-edge aliasing is excluded.
- **Paint:** The candy/pink/plum/champagne composition, side scroll band, door cameo, small rear ornament and bordered scallop layout are readable. The gold internal linework and cameo are simpler and coarser than the atlas. In the supplied `wheel-detail.png` and `cabin.png`, the paint grain reads as widespread pebbled roughness, especially across the lower pink body and door highlight, while the atlas reads as fine metallic sparkles beneath a smoother highlight. Lighting affects the apparent gloss and color, but the visible grain character remains a finish discrepancy.
- **Wheels/chrome/cabin:** Wire spokes, narrow whitewall, tread definition, lenses/bezels, round mirrors, six rear lamps, dense rear brightwork and plum benches read as finished game-model details. Upholstery channel ends still look rigid, the tire grooves are coarse against the atlas's finer road-tire character, and bumper corners are simplified. The small wired control object is readable in the cabin. The camera does not expose the instrument face sufficiently to infer missing gauges.
- **Mechanisms:** Frame-connected ram supports, cups/springs, separate dampers, axle/links, driveline, and mounted pumps/batteries form a visibly coherent assembly. Rounded bay covers and trunk housings relate to the surrounding lining, but their large plain surfaces and abrupt bases remain less integrated than the atlas installation. Static views do not establish travel, hose behavior, hidden connections, load capacity or manufacturing suitability.

## Remaining visible refinements

1. **Paint finish:** Reduce the broad pebbled appearance visible on the wheel-close body and cabin door. Preserve fine sparse flakes under a smooth clear-coat highlight; verify under the existing lighting and a neutral close view. Refine the internal fan/flower line hierarchy and cameo detail.
2. **Surface continuity:** Blend the lower stamping ends beside the front arch, outer hood/deck corners and body/bumper junctions. Integrate the thin roof-end perimeter with the pillars while retaining the reference hardtop opening.
3. **Close details:** Round and join upholstery channel ends, refine tire-groove scale, and shape bumper corners more closely to the atlas.
4. **Installation finish:** Blend the bases of the large curved bay/trunk covers into their surrounding lining and give the broad plain surfaces more of the atlas's formed installation character.

Intentional body/wheel removal, support stands and extended service poses received no defect penalty. Beige lighting, broad chrome reflections and 960×600 sampling are presentation limitations. This is visual acceptance under the fixed rubric, not proof of driving feel or moving mechanical clearance.
