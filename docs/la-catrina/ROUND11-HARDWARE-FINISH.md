# Round 11 integrated wheelhouse forms

The previous rounded rectangular ram caps remained visually separate from their wheelhouses. This revision replaces each cap outer wall and its adjacent wheelhouse roof with one continuous domed blister extending to the original X +/-0.930 m outer tub seam. The dome lies above the former safe curved wheelhouse roof. A 60 mm-wide elliptical inboard shoulder rises from the unchanged Y 0.796 m inner-wall seam to the existing ram crown. The rear crown also curves across the former flat span. End returns follow the curved profile and close against the retained inner returns and tub. These are replacement skins rather than overlays; the old perimeter bead is removed.

The main floor, maximum crown-height function, suspension/ram pivots, fixed pin centers, rail feet, exhaust and rear frame casings remain unchanged. The new profile enters the previous cap void near its inner upper corner and therefore requires the full dynamic component-clearance recheck; preservation of the crown or the outer seam alone is not approval.

Fixed ram support members now use tapered eight-sided closed sections. Their original endpoints, section envelope and end lands remain; their waist narrows to 70 percent of the end section. This is an original game-model fabrication interpretation, not a strength-qualified structural change.

Compilation and in-memory construction passed. All tested cover/tower face normals and vertex normals agree. Static exact-triangle SAT found zero cap contacts against 176 fixed tower/pin/spacer/inner-ball parts across 120 broad-phase pairs. Receipt: `build/la-catrina/round-11-hardware-static.json`. Native visual evaluation, serialized tire tests and the full loaded-pose mechanical check are separate required gates.
