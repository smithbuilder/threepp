# La Catrina — reference fidelity revision 2

The independent round-01 reviews scored the first model 4.275 and 3.76 out of 10. This revision responds to their shared findings. It is an authored revision, not an independent acceptance score.

## Coachwork

- Replace the extruded side profile with curved transverse stampings, shallow lower swages, a proud upper spear and rolled fender crowns.
- Use a low, flattened rear wheel opening to match the skirted quarter panel in the reference. Keep the front opening circular and retain the existing tire dimensions and moving wheel hierarchy.
- Give the hood and deck distinct inset boundaries, folded hems, formed underside ribs and rolled shoulders. Retain the opening hinge pivots and validated floors and bulkheads.
- Replace the thin planar roof with compound curvature, rolled edges, smoothly shaped rear sail panels, gently curved windshield/backlight and nested rubber/chrome glazing surrounds.
- Replace bumper tubes with continuous crowned pressed surfaces, rolled edges, wraparound ends and separate guards. Nest the four larger front lamps in formed bezels and add fluted reflectors. Add the ribbed rear insert, six concentric red lenses and individual bezels.

## Paint and trim

The large separate medallions are replaced with an original continuous fan lace. Fine radiating filaments, scalloped lines and branching scrolls form a small interlocked repeat. Hood and trunk use framed divided panels; the roof uses one uninterrupted panel. The side spear carries small scrolls and restrained cameos. All paint textures remain at or below 1024 pixels per side. No reference pixels were copied.

The candy material uses a low metallic response with a clear lacquer layer. Chrome remains a distinct polished-metal material. The plum upholstery is named vinyl, following the verified 1964-style seat construction described in the real-world basis document; it is not represented as bare metal or leather.

## Wheels and cabin

The four metric tires and hub pivots are retained. Each wire face now has two crossed spoke planes, 96 fine wires, threaded nipples, a deep formed rim, hub flanges, rolled lips, knockoff ears and a center medallion. The narrow whitewalls and tire contact dimensions remain unchanged.

The benches use curved indented pleated surfaces, piping, stitching and seatbelt buckles. Door cards gain pleats, carpet, window cranks and fasteners. The dashboard gains a radio scale, buttons, glovebox seam and lock, pedals and a rearview mirror.

## Implementation and verification scope

`scripts/catrina_art.py` installs the art construction functions into `scripts/catrina_generate.py`. `scripts/catrina_hardware.py` separately supplies researched wishbones, bushings, damper components and hydraulic fittings. `baja427_generate.py` supplies the shared primitive base. All four source files are named and hashed in the model manifest.

Source generation requires Python, NumPy, Pillow and SciPy. SciPy supplies the monotone cubic interpolation used for the stamped side-body cross section.

The revision remains an original game asset inspired by the supplied sheet, not manufacturer CAD. Static mesh checks and native loaded-rig checks are separate from visual acceptance. The independent reviewers must judge the actual captures against the original sheet before the requested 8/10 threshold can be claimed.

## Round-03 corrections from actual studio captures

The second review exposed serious presentation defects that numerical mesh validity alone did not catch. The C-pillar skins, corner returns and formed wheel barrels faced inward. The black headlamp backing cap sat ahead of the reflector center. The header ended higher than the hood, producing an unintended upturned lip. Those defects have been corrected directly, with new intended-surface-direction and reflector-occlusion checks in the root-owned audit.

Round03 also adds closed end returns and interior sill/kick/quarter panels, a rounded elliptical rear opening, broader pressed bumpers, convex sealed-beam cover surfaces, convex concentric tail lenses and a silver ribbed rear insert. The pink paint and gold artwork have stronger color separation, with reduced broad clearcoat washout and interlocked fan rows. This addresses observed image defects; independent acceptance remains pending.

## Subsequent reference-paint correction

Full-resolution inspection showed that the exact sheet uses one framed trunk field, two framed hood fields, and unframed roof lace. The generic vector fan repeat still differed. The current asset therefore uses rectified paint fields derived from the supplied reference, with explicit source and processing provenance in [REFERENCE-PAINT.md](REFERENCE-PAINT.md) and the asset JSON. The earlier statements about no reference pixels apply only to the superseded round01–03 texture versions. Geometry and the small vector cameos remain original. The current side-spear scroll is also derived from a source mask, as documented in the later revisions.

## Round04 formed-panel correction

The hood now has paired crowns and a narrow raised central spine, with distinct raised fender shoulders. The nose and deck roll down through a 160mm transition into their end faces. Closed end hems join the outer skin to the formed inner skin. Actual 20mm-deep lower-side recesses replace the earlier suggestion of stamped panels through lines alone; the rockers ease upward at the corners.

Front and rear bumpers now use a smoothly varying pressed section, 1.99m overall span, 205mm corner returns, a recessed center license pocket and shaped lower edges. Headlamp pair pockets are 240mm high. Wire spokes use a readable 3.6mm diameter and retain the independent rim/hub/cone construction. The opening lids use four pressed inner relief wells with a perimeter flange, center spine, cross-web, hinge leaves and pins; the previous parallel reinforcing bars were removed.

The curved hood/deck ends intentionally have surface normals below the old flat-panel threshold of 0.98 in Y. Their minimum upward components are approximately 0.938 and 0.952; winding, finite/unit normals, and the other asset checks remain separate requirements.

## Round05 corrections from rendered evidence

The lower-side recesses were reduced to shallow 4.5mm stampings after the independent reviewers identified the deep rectangular depressions as unlike the sheet. The rear opening now uses a low flattened arch with its crown 170mm above the rear hub. The front opening radius is 315mm. The inward arch return is 27mm wide; the root-owned clearance audit tested the exterior as well as the inner enclosures across 243 actual native poses with 6mm tire allowance.

The rear dark header slot was traced to a liner protruding beyond the painted header, including a CPU ray intersection diagnosis. The liner was moved inboard. The wheel lacing uses tangentially opposed cones rather than radial spokes. Clearcoat and chrome responses were adjusted after testing the actual native lighting; the coating approximation represents candy pigment over a metallic base under clear lacquer.

## Round06 measured architecture and continuity

Full-resolution orthographic crops revealed larger proportion errors than small detail could solve. The front/rear overhang ratio in the side reference is approximately 175:245 image pixels. The exterior ends therefore move 190mm rearward while wheel centers and suspension hardpoints stay fixed. The hardtop moves 230mm rearward and the cabin fittings 150mm rearward. The door seam span is shortened to 1.28m, around the already-correct handle position. The separate hardware helper adjusts the bay boundaries, equipment rack, radiator support and weather seal to those choices. This is reference-derived game geometry, not a claim of factory dimensional accuracy.

The side molding and cameo move to 630mm above ground, preserving the approximately 150mm handle-to-molding gap visible in the reference. Deck shoulders and side skins now share exactly the same boundary and a matched tangent, eliminating the previous 25mm overlapping edge. Both quarter windows are shown lowered for the continuous hardtop opening in the sheet.

The front and rear upper fascia edges now dip in the center and rise around the lamps. Their chrome surrounds and inset surfaces follow that contour. The front grille uses horizontal-dominant ribs. The rear insert uses three pressed center channels. Tail lamps have a dark ruby outer zone, a small bright inner ring and a separate central red dome. The front bumper gains its three pressed center ribs, and both bumpers have the reference's lower center edge and continuous wrapped ends. The front center no longer carries an invented black license plate.

Rendered crossed spokes were partly hidden by an outward-projecting opaque brake-drum cap; that cap is now inboard of the hub. Bench pleats fade into smooth upper and lower edges. The wheel centers, separate steering/spin hierarchy, suspension pivots and tire envelope remain unchanged.

The generator now supports a geometry-bound cavity cache through `scripts/catrina_ao.py`. It appends vertex color only when the cache's geometry signature matches. The root-owned CPU bake separates static body, wheels and opening lids; moving suspension is excluded. This supplies local contact shading without painting false shadows into the reference artwork. Current author-source provenance lists six Python sources, including this export helper. Visual acceptance still requires independent review of the newly rendered model.
