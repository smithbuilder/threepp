# Fabricated suspension frame pockets

The model's original straight axle-station rails passed through the lower
spring seats. The revised boxed-steel route retains every suspension and
hydraulic hardpoint and all four ram-tower foot stations. It is an original
game-model fabrication interpretation, not a manufacturer chassis or a
structural-strength certification.

- Front pocket: 65 × 100 mm section, centered at |X| 0.370 m and Y 0.660 m.
  Closed welded entrances join the retained rail stations and their inboard
  web doublers. The engine isolator/boss advances 35 mm on the same cast bank
  onto a connected cradle; the engine and suspension pivots stay fixed.
- Front arm supports: paired 26 mm axial × 42 mm vertical tabs attach to the
  invariant cross-shaft ends and the retained rails. This section clears the
  adjacent formed arm channels; shaft and retaining-fastener contact is the
  intended attachment.
- Center waist: its half-width stays 220 mm through Z −0.200 m, replacing the
  previous 120 mm waist that intruded into the live slip-shaft sweep.
- Rear load path: 50 × 60 mm outriggers run under the cabin to |X| 0.795 m;
  matching risers pass beside the rear bench to Y 0.770 m. A 60 × 110 mm
  header connects to a 95 × 110 mm high inboard rail at |X| 0.270 m.
  Aft headers bridge into the existing two ram-tower posts and retained tail
  rail feet. This avoids descending through the live axle, Panhard rod,
  upper link, shocks, or spring-seat sweep.
- Hardware-authored enclosed floor channels follow those members. The main
  trunk-floor height, exterior panels, wheelhouses' outer boundaries, wheel
  centers, and ram-tower feet are unchanged. Local exhaust bends route around
  the new steel; the existing engine and tailpipe endpoints remain.

The author check reconstructs the native wheel transforms from the pose CSV,
including 81 distinct suspension pairs, three actuator commands, and all 243
steered poses. It tests actual moving triangles against conservative closed
frame boxes, and sampled frame surfaces against tires with the existing 6 mm
allowance. Its scope includes arms, uprights, axle/differential, all rear
links, dampers/rods, hydraulic barrels/rods, coils/seats, and the rigid/sliding
propeller shaft. The boxes include the volume removed by rendered edge bevels.

This document records the construction, not final approval. The combined
export, independent exact-triangle attachment/casing check, serialized tire
audit, native review, and visual scores are recorded separately by the parent
task. Deliberate shaft-end attachment contacts are distinguished from moving
channel interference; this is not a claim that every pair in the vehicle has
been structurally or dynamically certified.
