# La Catrina — mechanical presentation adapter

`showcase/CatrinaRig.hpp` presents the shared vehicle simulation's chassis pose, wheel travel, steering, wheel rotation and hydraulic command using the named groups in the GLB. The shared Bullet simulation supplies takeoff, pitch, tire unloading and landing; the adapter does not prescribe a separate chassis-bounce animation.

## Moving assemblies

The front corners retain two parallel 0.535 m arms, vertically separated by 0.18 m, with frame pivots at X ±0.18 m. Complete arm assemblies rotate without changing length, and the steering upright stays connected to the moving ball joints. Their channel sections, bushes, cross shafts, boots and fasteners are authored in `scripts/catrina_hardware.py`. The equal-length parallel geometry is a game approximation, not a measured production Impala alignment curve.

The rear axle is one rigid group with a 1.51 m hub span. A closure solve finds its lateral and longitudinal position, yaw and pitch for the two requested wheel heights while preserving the two lower links, upper link and Panhard bar. Both rear wheel groups inherit that axle pose. Nominal hub height is 0.2891 m; allowed body-relative heights are −0.12 to 0.42 m at the front and −0.28 to 0.42 m at the rear.

The requested displacement comes from the 0.58 m wheel-rate equivalent command multiplied by 0.42. The visible ram stops at its mechanical travel and at the spring compression limit, while the simulation retains the requested pressure/preload command. This is an explicit game approximation: visual actuator stroke does not equal every commanded displacement. Cup distance is limited by the **signed** top-to-seat distance minus 0.114 m; an absolute length cannot hide an inverted spring. The shorter casing keeps the cup below the gland, and the rigid rod retains at least 0.060 m engagement at its extension stop. Its theoretical mechanical minimum gives 0.005 m upper-head clearance and 0.016 m cup-to-gland clearance; the sampled permitted poses retain about 0.01098 m above the rod tip. Wheel contacts, linkage pivots and chassis physics are unchanged.

The four lower spring seats have independent `coil_i_seat` nodes. They follow the solved spring bottoms with unit scale; only the coil wire deforms vertically. Damper eyes use fixed pins and spherical inner bearings on `damper_i_top_joint` and `damper_i_lower_joint`. Their pins share the actual clevis axes. A shared eye center alone is no longer treated as proof that a pin fits its fork.

## Evidence and checks

The native CPU audit loads the actual GLB and exercises **243 poses**: nine left-travel values, nine right-travel values and three steering positions, including maximum hydraulic command. It checks finite transforms, wheel/hub attachment, fixed link lengths, axle span, requested hub heights, signed spring separation/solid clearance, hydraulic rod engagement, cup-to-casing clearance, damper pin centers/axes and bearing angles, rigid metal world scales, fixed driveshaft member lengths/engagement, lower-seat attachment, matching drum/wheel rotation and panel operation. It returns to nominal ride height to check authored wheel coordinates.

Each session writes `physics-audit/catrina-rig-audit.json` and `physics-audit/catrina-rig-poses.csv`. The CSV contains the solved position and orientation of all four wheels for every pose: 972 rows before equivalent transforms are deduplicated. `build/suspension-playground/latest-audit.json` identifies the last shared-physics audit session, executable hash and check count. Read those receipts with the current model manifest; an older successful session is not automatically evidence for revised assets or executables.

`scripts/catrina_enclosure_audit.py` uses the exported transforms to test selected serialized floor, bulkhead, wheelhouse, liner and equipment surfaces. It samples triangle interiors at no more than **25 mm spacing** against tire cylinders expanded by **6 mm**. `build/la-catrina/enclosure-audit.json` records the model hash, pose source, sampled parts, minimum cylinder metric and intersections. This is a targeted sampled clearance check, not a whole-vehicle or continuous moving-mesh collision certificate.

## Fidelity and acceptance

Visible links follow fixed-length arcs that introduce lateral and longitudinal hub motion; the simplified suspension contact queries remain straight rays. Their vertical travel agrees, but this is not a full multibody suspension or hydraulic-fluid simulation. Hose flex, spring stress, structural loads, detailed spline engagement and real-road handling are outside these checks. Construction and material references are documented in [REAL-WORLD-BASIS.md](REAL-WORLD-BASIS.md).

The independent static reference-sheet gate is recorded separately in `build/la-catrina/independent/accepted-model.json`: reviewer A **8.020/10**, reviewer B **8.115/10**, both on the exact model hash above and the same 16-view inspection set. Those scores do not certify continuous motion, physical-keyboard feel, structural safety or manufacturing suitability. Remaining visible limitations are retained in both review reports and [MODEL.md](MODEL.md).
