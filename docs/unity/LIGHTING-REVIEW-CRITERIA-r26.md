# Independent evening-lighting criteria: r26

November 11, 2026, Pacific. Preparation for the requested roughly 6 PM city, before dark, with colourful sunset and streetlights beginning to illuminate the street. This evaluator has not implemented the new profile or launched a player/editor. This document defines a review; it does not approve an unseen candidate or prescribe a passing score.

## Baseline and intended improvement

The actual r25 [garage](../../evidence/cinder-unity-r25/review/01-garage.png) and [Nocturne street view](../../evidence/cinder-unity-r25/review/06-nocturne-roof.png) have pale blue-grey sky, largely neutral road/facades and little visible lighting contribution from the existing fixtures. Their readable paint, lace, chrome and cabin forms are preservation references. The r25 lighting review remains 7/10 and city art 6/10; neither is the target score for this new scene.

The new art direction should be visibly coherent at the ordinary 960 x 600 gameplay view. A warm peach/coral horizon, lavender transition and cooler blue upper sky should influence car reflection gradients and the balance of sun-facing versus shaded surfaces. Sufficient cool ambient fill should keep brick, foliage, road repairs, kerbs and shaded vehicle forms readable. These are visual relationships, not astronomical claims about a particular date/location at 18:00.

The street should still read as daylight fading toward evening. Its main forms must remain readable between lamps. Lamps should add local warm accents as they begin to matter; they should not be the only reason a car or road is visible. A uniform orange cast, dark blue night, neon sky with unchanged midday ground, or brighter bloom over unchanged lighting would not meet the request.

## Finite visual gates

1. **Sky and illumination agree.** Inspect the sunset direction and opposite direction. The visible bright horizon, directional key, reflective hood/roof and SH fill should describe the same environment. No separate old daylight reflection or second bright sun should remain. Sky seams, cubemap edge steps and broad banding are defects, not an acceptable consequence of low resolution.
2. **Material identities survive.** Catrina remains rose with gold lace; Luna remains emerald; Nocturne retains blue-grey separation rather than becoming a black silhouette. Chrome must retain shape and highlights instead of a continuous white strip. At least one shaded flank and one dark cabin view must retain their local boundaries. New lamp bloom must not obscure lace, steering/dashboard shapes or fixture housings.
3. **Existing fixtures cause actual illumination.** The luminous source belongs to the underside of the authored head, not to the pole centre or open air. A matched lamp-on/off pair must show a localized change on actual road/pavement surfaces. The contribution should follow distance, surface orientation and a plausible downward beam. A decal-like disc that paints all materials identically, floats above the road, crosses height discontinuities unchanged or remains equally bright facing away from the fixture fails this gate. If lamps are unshadowed, avoid claiming blocked-light fidelity; inspect leakage under cars and through nearby facades.
4. **No lighting pops while driving.** A bounded approach/passage/departure take should show stable fixture emission and smooth light selection/falloff. Inspect shiny paint for repeated sky IBL, hard selection boundaries and pumping; inspect lamp edges and bloom for shimmer. A few sampled stills cannot establish continuous stability.
5. **Scene-wide improvement is visible.** A normal city overview and normal garage/gameplay view must show the intended sunset without diagnostic exposure, hidden-material controls or a special sky-only camera. Retain the readable HUD/menu. Update visible time-of-day copy if the selected city is now evening.

## Source-grounded risks to resolve before capture

- `StandaloneHost.cs` currently authors the old afternoon directional sun, pale fog, original panorama, native reflection cube and ambient SH separately. Switch these as one explicit city profile. Reflection strength's gamma-encoded Unity API call and `lightsUseLinearIntensity=true` are calibrated transport rules; changing artistic radiance is not a reason to undo those conversions. Preserve the existing NativeTone and PBR material factors.
- `CinderSkyBake.cs` supplies the already-oriented panorama, a 128-face-size filtered cube and ambient SH. The new sunset needs one consistent directional convention across all three. Apply the longitude correction/rotation once. Keep source/profile hashes, cardinal samples, finite checks and white-furnace/hemisphere tests. White-furnace success alone does not prove the sunset brightness, direction or colour is appropriate.
- The existing native environment policy leaves the original sun in visible sky and cube mip 0, clamps it out of rough mips/diffuse, and lets the explicit directional light supply the key. A new authored disc/halo must not be convolved into diffuse and then counted again at full strength as a directional key. Do not blindly reuse a noon extraction result for a new environment. If the new environment has no discrete sun, document that policy instead of asserting native source parity.
- `CinderBody.shader`'s `LightingCinder` unconditionally samples the sky cube for clearcoat. Additional pixel-light ForwardAdd passes can therefore repeat indirect coat energy unless guarded to the base pass. Existing one-directional r25 captures did not exercise that case. Check generated additive HLSL and a matched lamp-on/off car region away from any plausible lamp contribution. Direct coat light from the extra lamp must remain; indirect sky coat must be added once.
- `QualitySettings.asset` currently selects tier 5, whose `pixelLightCount` is 4; other tiers have fewer. Explicitly bound lamp policy rather than relying on an inherited setting or assuming that eight authored fixtures all become pixel lights. Per-renderer light selection can pop, and large combined city meshes can receive costly additional passes. Inspect actual GPU output and measured draws. If a bounded analytic road-light path is used, it still must obey source position, distance and surface normal; it is not occlusion-aware GI and must not be described as such.
- Existing lamps are already authored in frozen `assets/unity/city/source/CityBlock.hpp`: head centre is `(x+sin(yaw)*1.35, 5.24, z+cos(yaw)*1.35)`, with x in `{-23.7,23.7,-44.7,44.7}`, z in `{49,85}`, yaw -pi/2 on negative x and +pi/2 on positive x. There are eight fixture heads. The city is batched, so adding a light per mesh node is not a reliable fixture inventory. Bind light/emission positions through the module frame; do not relocate the donor geometry to fit a desired pool.
- `CinderCity.shader` preserves frozen vertex shade in albedo. A different sun angle cannot erase or fully regenerate that static shade. `CinderView.cs` also disables city shadow casting and lets only ground receive the existing car shadows. Low-angle sunlight will not make buildings cast new long shadows under the present contract. Judge the resulting visual compromise honestly; do not sell it as complete dusk relighting or spend additional shadow maps beyond the cap.
- The r25 bloom strength 0.10 is an upper bound from its retention review, not permission to raise it for lamp visibility. Large local dark-cabin spill already existed. Treat lamp HDR radiance and extraction together, with emission-on/light-off controls if needed. Avoid solving a weak lamp by globally brightening the glow or changing donor roughness/metalness.

The implementer's proposed response is a separate sunset city static-shade bake plus eight bounded shadowless analytic spot contributions evaluated within the existing base pass, without Unity extra-light components. This avoids the proposed ForwardAdd cost but has not yet been reviewed in source or pixels. The shared light evaluation must consume the actual sampled PBR surface and correctly transformed world-space normal/view, smoothly fade at its range/cone boundary, and preserve the body clearcoat energy split. Direct lamp radiance should not be attenuated by indirect-only AO. A downward cone and fixture ceiling do not constitute occlusion; inspect wall, kerb and parked-car leakage. Any extra per-pixel visibility work needs an explicit bounded model and measured cost. Load one city profile at a time; a separate bake must not accidentally duplicate the city geometry. Preserve the original static shade as the daylight control, and bind the new bake's direction to its actual sunset sun.

## Resource and scope boundaries

Retain 960 x 600/30, no MSAA, one 512-pixel shadow map and the 2 GiB job. The r25 matched benchmark recorded 219 draws and 693,585 active geometry triangles: fewer than 6,500 triangles remain below the 700,000 active-geometry cap, and added light passes consume the draw margin. Prefer reusing authored geometry and bounded shadowless light contributions. Existing active geometry counts do not measure all-pass submitted geometry; keep that distinction explicit.

Require a same-scene bounded performance comparison and real runtime light/pass receipts. A capped PNG-IO run cannot establish spare GPU capacity. Preserve failed candidates and matched controls. No simulation, native donor material, handling or broader art acceptance follows from the lighting revision.
