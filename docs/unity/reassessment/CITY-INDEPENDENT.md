# Independent city and environment reassessment

Reviewer: fresh environment-art evaluator, November 9, 2026. Scope: existing r9 captures, native Mercer reference captures and their owning source. No implementation, editor, game, render or graphics launch was performed. Previous scores do not determine this verdict.

## Verdict

**The Unity city is visibly a step backward. It preserves much of Mercer's physical block layout but replaces its visual construction with a substantially simplified city kit. Lighting and material response amplify that loss. This is not explained by a different engine or camera alone.**

My absolute environment-art judgment is **3.5/10 for the shown Unity r9 environment** and **5.5/10 for the shown native Mercer environment**. These are qualitative judgments of the available images, not instrument readings or whole-game scores. Native Mercer is itself a repetitive, sparsely inhabited prototype; it is a useful restoration baseline, not a finished commercial-art target. The Unity environment is not ready to be described as a visual improvement or a presentation-approved replacement for it.

The cars require separate assessment. Here, their street context fails to celebrate them: a dark foreground, bright flat sidewalk, blank shop glazing, sparse angular trees and missing distant city make highly detailed cars look pasted into an unfinished stage. For slow cruising and hydraulic display, near-field street quality matters more than it would behind a fast racing camera.

UV/material parity needs care: the r9 boxes do have metre-derived UVs (`create_kit.py:26–32`); this is **not** a claim that Unity has no UVs. Its general 0.35 UV/metre rule also differs from native per-material tiling (brick 1.65 m/tile, plaster 5 m/tile, asphalt 4.5 m/tile). Native building finish assignments come from `CityLayout.hpp`; Unity instead cycles `brick[j%3]`, changing which facade is brick, buff or plaster. The original licensed photographic maps are partly reused, but retaining a texture file does not retain scale, roughness, surface construction or neighborhood identity.

## Five repairs, in order

1. **Restore the native city content contract in the Unity authoring source.** Four-sided openings, actual facade depth, window treatments, roof/parapet/service elements, correct per-building finish assignments, visible boundary and context shells. Keep the native donor untouched. Start with one complete coffee/workshop street segment and prove it at car height before replicating the block.
2. **Establish lighting/material parity on that segment.** Use the same sky rotation and compatible sun direction; restore calibrated roughness/normal handling for road, pavement, brick, stone and bark with material-specific metre scale. Balance sun, ambient and grade so shaded cars remain legible and bright trim retains detail. A render-pipeline change is not justified by this evidence.
3. **Restore a coherent cruising street.** Double-yellow hierarchy, junction approaches, parking, kerb stones, drainage and restrained repairs/wear. Preserve the authoritative collision surface. Road design should support strolling-speed car appreciation, not add racing gates or spectacle unrelated to the brief.
4. **Restore trees and human-scale street dressing.** Recover clustered broadleaf silhouette, bark and canopy light/shadow coherence, then benches, signs, hydrants, bins and pocket planting. Judge at the intended closest approach; do not hide low-detail foliage behind distance-only shots.
5. **Compose one convincing lowrider gathering destination.** Make the workshop/coffee frontage an attractive place to park, watch hydraulics and inspect coachwork, with a readable shop entrance, deliberate parking/display area and an unobstructed car-height viewing position. This is a new art-direction task beyond native parity: the native scene also lacks strong gathering culture. Do not expand the map before this one destination works.

The next independent review must evaluate the final images on their own merits; reaching a score threshold or passing a simulation probe will not resolve these art blockers.

This is a still/source-based reassessment. It establishes concrete loss of environment content and visible presentation problems. It does not establish physical input feel, motion aliasing over a complete route, listened audio, high-resolution performance, or player enjoyment.
